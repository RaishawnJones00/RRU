Welcome to Restitution Revival United
We are a nondenominational organization committed to faith, unity, and spiritual growth.

Restitution Revival was established in 1973 by Prophet Vernon Dale Heslep and Mrs. Emma Jo Heslep. Our ministry has deep prophetic and evangelistic roots.

Pastor Sha-Ron Heslep now leads as the new pastor and overseer. Our mission is to help others come to Jesus and to continue building God’s kingdom.

You are always welcome to come and worship with us. We aim to show the love of God through every interaction.
